# Evening and Yin Yoga — Calming the Body and Nervous System
category: yoga/wieczorna
tags: yoga, yin, evening, calming, nervous system, relaxation, sleep
source: https://www.youtube.com/@yogaexhto

## How Agnieszka opens a practice

- [ ] **Gathering attention** — cross-legged seat, eyes closed, "slowly we gather our attention into the here and now"
- [ ] **Contact with the mat and gravity** — feeling the sit bones and feet on the mat, a heavy lower body; from that base the crown of the head reaches toward the ceiling
- [ ] **Elevated hips** — sitting on a blanket, pillow or bolster to lengthen the spine
- [ ] **Face scan** — "we don't clench the jaw"; relaxed forehead and the point between the eyebrows

## What yin means in her sessions

- [ ] **Long holds** — "we'll stay in them for a while, to regenerate our nervous system"; on your own you can stay 5–10 minutes
- [ ] **Props instead of forcing** — blanket under the knees, pillow/book/block under the forehead, a bolster to hug
- [ ] **Surrendering to gravity** — "we allow ourselves to trust gravity"; movement happens by itself through releasing tension
- [ ] **Emotions in the hips** — "we hold a lot of uncomfortable emotions in the hips"; if tears or anger surface: "they will pass, they will dissolve together with that tension"

## Recurring poses and their purpose

- [ ] **Child's pose** — opening or settling; breath into the back and hips
- [ ] **Cat–cow** — the inhale opens the chest, the exhale rounds the spine
- [ ] **Viparita karani (legs up the wall)** — "a pose that remarkably regulates the nervous system"; after a long day, for heavy legs, before sleep
- [ ] **Yin hip openers** — low lunge with the foot at the mat's edge, sinking onto the elbows; knee to the mat's edge, forehead on the forearms
- [ ] **Butterfly with a fold** — soles of the feet together, forehead on a block
- [ ] **Passive forward fold** — knees bent, the back may round, "we let the head hang loose"
- [ ] **Reclined twists** — knees drop to one side, shoulder near the mat; "let the organs cleanse themselves"
- [ ] **Neck release** — the ear drops toward the shoulder without lifting it; "gravity will do it for us"
- [ ] **Savasana** — the body becomes heavy part by part and sinks into the mat

## Breath cues

- [ ] **Three-part breath (wave)** — inhaling "as if we were filling a glass of water": belly, ribs, chest; exhale in reverse
- [ ] **Belly breathing** — hands on the belly, "the belly opens like a balloon"
- [ ] **Lengthened exhale** — the tool of letting go: "with the exhales we release tension"
- [ ] **No straining** — a full breath, but not so deep that it tenses the chest, shoulders, neck
- [ ] **The mindfulness muscle** — when attention drifts, "we bring it back, with a smile on the face, to the present moment"

## Video sessions

- [ ] **Evening Yoga, 15 min** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tq-9frr6yho) — can be done in bed: three-part breath, child's pose, seated twists, viparita karani, reclined twists; ends with settling into sleep

- [ ] **Yoga – 15 Minutes of Relaxation** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qtu_FQAPhPU) — a reset "for when you simply want to catch your breath": mouth exhale "as if fogging up a window", shoulder rolls, neck, cat–cow, child's pose, passive fold
- [ ] **18 min for a slow morning or evening** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MO62fTg_1Nw) — for every level: child's pose, cat–cow, neck, lunges, viparita karani with butterfly, breath observation with hands on the belly
- [ ] **Gentle 15-minute sequence** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZoFD4d3adXA) — the most movement-based: puppy pose, sphinx, downward dog, warrior II, seated folds, half bridge, happy baby, savasana

## Notes

- Transitions stay slow; after savasana, rolling to the side, the head rises last.
- Closing: inhale, arms up, exhale, hands to the heart, "thank yourself", a smile, "Namaste".
- Repeated reassurance: "everything is exactly as it should be".
- After the gentle sequence she suggests meditation or breathing practices.
