# Yoga: 5 Minutes Of Mindfulness
category: yoga/sekwencje
tags: yoga, awareness, mindfulness, short practice, breath, cat cow
source: https://exhto.substack.com/p/joga-5-minut-uwaznosci

## Idea
A short, 5-minute sequence that gathers your attention and brings it into the present moment. Full video session on YouTube (Yoga Exhto).

## Sequence
- [ ] **Start — tabletop.** Hands under the shoulders, knees under the hips. A calm inhale, a long exhale. No rush — this time is for you.
- [ ] **Cow–Cat.** Exhale: round the back, chin to chest. Inhale: open the chest. Flowing movement, led by the breath.
- [ ] **Balance — activating the center.** From a neutral position, reach the left arm forward and the right leg back. Pause, breathe. If you lose your balance — that's okay: come back, try again. Switch sides.
- [ ] **Downward-facing dog.** Tuck the toes, lift the hips. Bend the knees slightly, relax the neck. Deep inhale, long exhale.
- [ ] **Forward fold.** Walk the feet forward, hang. Let the head be heavy; you can hold your elbows. Release the tension.
- [ ] **Finale.** Roll up vertebra by vertebra. Inhale: arms up. Exhale: hands to the heart. Close your eyes, stand quietly.

## Affirmation
"I am calm. I am steady."

## Notes
This is about finding stability within yourself, not about perfect poses. Thank yourself for this moment of mindful movement.
