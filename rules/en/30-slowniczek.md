# A Glossary of Holistic Terms
category: health/wellness
tags: glossary, TCM, ayurveda, yoga, basics, terms

## Idea
A short guide to the terms that appear in my posts and recipes — so nobody feels lost. Come back here whenever you meet an unfamiliar word.

## Medicine and traditions
- [ ] **TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine)** — a thousands-of-years-old Chinese art of caring for health: it sees the body as a whole, with energy (qi) flowing through meridians. This is where my foot soaks, warming foods and autumn lung care come from.
- [ ] **Ayurveda** — traditional Indian medicine, "the science of life". It teaches that each of us has a different constitution and needs different food, daily rhythm and practices.
- [ ] **Dosha** — in Ayurveda: the three energies that build a person’s constitution — vata (movement), pitta (fire), kapha (stability). Their balance = wellbeing.
- [ ] **Qi (pronounced "chee")** — in TCM: the life energy circulating in the body. When it flows freely we feel good; stagnation shows up as tension and fatigue.
- [ ] **Yin and yang** — two complementary qualities: yin (coolness, calm, regeneration) and yang (warmth, movement, activity). In the kitchen and on the mat we look for their balance.
- [ ] **Meridians** — in TCM: the channels qi flows through. I work with them e.g. by tapping in my morning energy routine.

## Yoga
- [ ] **Asana** — a yoga pose. It doesn’t have to look perfect; it’s meant to serve you.
- [ ] **Savasana** — the relaxation pose at the end of practice, lying on your back. It integrates the whole session in the body.
- [ ] **Yin yoga** — a very calm style of yoga: poses held long (3–10 minutes), with pillows and blankets; it works deeply on fascia and the nervous system.
- [ ] **Yoga Nidra** — a guided deep-relaxation practice done lying down, on the border of sleep and waking.
- [ ] **Nadi Shodhana** — alternate-nostril breathing; calming and balancing.
- [ ] **RYT200 / Yoga Alliance** — an international yoga teacher certificate after a minimum of 200 hours of training, issued by Yoga Alliance. I hold one.

## Kitchen
- [ ] **Kitchari** — an Ayurvedic dish of rice and mung beans with spices; easy to digest, cleansing, the staple of healing cooking.
- [ ] **Ghee** — clarified butter; a fat prized in Ayurveda for supporting digestion.
- [ ] **Agni** — in Ayurveda: the "digestive fire", the strength of your digestion. Weak agni = heaviness after meals; we support it with warm food and spices.
- [ ] **CCF infusion** — a tea of cumin, coriander and fennel; gentle on the belly, supports digestion.

## Everyday life
- [ ] **Mindfulness** — the skill of being here and now, without judging. We train it with breath, meditation and mindful movement.
- [ ] **Affirmation** — a short, positive sentence repeated consciously, e.g. "I am calm. I am steady." It helps retune your inner dialogue.
