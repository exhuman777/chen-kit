# Yoga for Shoulders, Wrists and Tension Release
category: yoga/napiecie
tags: yoga, shoulders, arms, wrists, tension, stress, yin
source: https://www.youtube.com/@yogaexhto

## Where the tension comes from and why these sessions

- [ ] **Overworked hands and wrists** — Agnieszka addresses people who "don't want or can't use their hands and wrists too much, because they're overworked or have some injuries".
- [ ] **A day of sitting, travel** — the hands-free session is "ideal for the morning, but also great after work, after a day of sitting or after a journey".
- [ ] **Tension hides in the face** — every session repeats the cue: check you're not clenching the jaw, relax the forehead and "the point between the brows".
- [ ] **Strong arms for the holidays** — with humor: we need them "to lift all those platters, pots, presents, to hug each other".

## Recurring poses and cues

- [ ] **Thread the needle (yin)** — from tabletop, arm out to the side on the inhale, under the other arm on the exhale, head on the forearm; "the exhales take away all the tension".
- [ ] **Child's pose with a bolster** — forehead or ear on the support, "inhales into the lower spine", everything sinks to the mat on the exhale.
- [ ] **Reclined twist** — knees to one side, opposite shoulder glued to the mat; blanket or bolster under the knees if the shoulder lifts.
- [ ] **Wrist warm-up** — pressing palm against palm in namaste plus the "zombie move": hands up on the inhale, flicking the fingers, "to keep things as safe as possible".
- [ ] **Strengthening** — push-ups on the knees (ankles crossed), plank, side plank ("first fall? no worries, we get back up"), cobra with lifted hands, dolphin on the forearms.
- [ ] **Standing sequence without hands** — coming up to standing through a squat, rolling up vertebra by vertebra; warrior II, triangle (hand on the calf, a block or a book), goddess ("we smile at ourselves, it helps us get through it"), chair, malasana.
- [ ] **Breath leads the movement** — the inhale lengthens the spine and opens the chest, the exhale deepens the pose; "with every exhale feel gravity working harder".
- [ ] **Fold from the bottom up** — "the forward fold starts from the back, not from the head; the head lengthens last and drops last".

## Sessions (mini-summaries)

- [ ] **Yin Yoga for the shoulders, 17 min** — passive session for the upper body: child's pose on a bolster, thread the needle both sides, chest opening toward the mat, reclined twists, savasana; slow pace, everything happens on the exhale (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zT9tFpiM5eY).
- [ ] **Pre-holiday session for the shoulders, 17 min** — strengthening: wrist warm-up, knee to elbow, push-ups, planks, cobra, downward dog, dolphin; at the end we tense the whole body on the inhale and let everything go in savasana; "let our arms know something's coming" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYd4zZ7aV2c).
- [ ] **A day off for the wrists and hands, 27 min** — the whole practice without leaning on the hands: seated cat-cow, side bends and twists, warriors, triangle, goddess, chair, malasana, seated folds; at the end we check whether the hands feel "tingling, vibration, warmth" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKTseEXlzrw).

## Props

- [ ] **Bolster, blanket or pillow** — under the torso in child's pose, under the knees in the twist; you can also practice with nothing.
- [ ] **Yoga block or a book** — under the hand in triangle, "so you reach the pose and feel comfortable in it".

## Notes

The common thread of all three sessions: shoulder tension releases not through force but through the exhale, gravity and a relaxed face. Strong accents (push-ups, dolphin) always get balanced with rest in child's pose and a full savasana. She closes with gratitude: "thank you so much for practicing together" and "smile at yourself, you've done a great job".
