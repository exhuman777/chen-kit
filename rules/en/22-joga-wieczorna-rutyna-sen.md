# Evening Yoga Routine For Better Sleep
category: yoga/sen
tags: yoga, sleep, evening, winding down, breath, yin, nervous system
source: https://exhto.substack.com/p/wieczorna-rutyna-na-lepszy-sen

## Idea
Better Sleep Yoga — a way to calm the nervous system, release the body, and prepare for sleep. The whole routine takes 20–30 minutes.

## 1. Calming the breath (4 min)
- [ ] Position: a comfortable cross-legged seat or reclined Supta Baddha Konasana (2 min).
- [ ] Breath: Box Breathing 4–4–4–4 — inhale 4 s, hold 4 s, exhale 4 s, hold 4 s (2 min).

## 2. Releasing the body (5–7 min, 1–2 min per pose)
- [ ] Child's Pose (Balasana) — releases the back and shoulders.
- [ ] Cat–Cow (Marjaryasana–Bitilasana) — gently warms up the spine.
- [ ] Reclined twist (Supta Matsyendrasana) — opens the hips and melts tension from the lower back.
- [ ] Legs up the wall (Viparita Karani) — improves circulation and relaxes.

## 3. Deep opening and grounding (5–8 min)
- [ ] Happy Baby (Ananda Balasana) — stretches the lower body.
- [ ] Supta Baddha Konasana — wonderful with pillows under the knees.
- [ ] Supported bridge (Setu Bandhasana with a bolster under the hips) — a gentle chest opening that settles the breath.

## 4. Finale — Savasana / meditation (5–10 min)
- [ ] Lie down on your back and cover yourself with a blanket.
- [ ] Yoga Nidra or a guided meditation, or the affirmation: "I am safe. My body is resting."

## 5. Atmosphere
- [ ] Dimmed lights, a candle, lavender or sandalwood oil.
- [ ] Soft ambient music, playlists like "Yoga Nidra Sleep", "Deep Rest".
- [ ] Nadi Shodhana breath (alternate nostril breathing) — quiets the body.
