# 7 Poses I Keep Coming Back To
category: yoga/asany
tags: yoga, asanas, calm, recovery, stress, breath
source: https://exhto.substack.com/p/7-pozycji-do-ktorych-wracam-gdy-potrzebuje

## Idea
Yoga doesn't always mean a 90-minute practice. Sometimes it's a few conscious breaths in one particular pose that helps you come back to yourself. These asanas work: they ground, soothe, open, and restore you after stress, sitting, and rushing around.

## Poses
- [ ] **Child's Pose (Balasana)** — quieting and recovery. It releases the back, calms the nervous system, and gives a sense of safety. It teaches that rest is part of the practice too. Worth staying in a little longer.
- [ ] **Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana)** — draws the whole body into the present moment. It stretches the back of the body, moves energy, and creates space to return to the breath. A "restart" button after a hard day.
- [ ] **Lunge** — opens the hips after long hours of sitting. It releases tension and stress stored in the hips.
- [ ] **Bridge (Setu Bandhasana)** — a shot of energy. It opens the chest while both restoring and energizing.
- [ ] **Tree (Vrksasana)** — physical and emotional balance. Different every time, because it perfectly mirrors the state we're in.
- [ ] **Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana)** — a moment of quiet, best in its Yin version. It stretches the back and teaches letting go.
- [ ] **Savasana** — integrates the whole practice in the body. They say it's the hardest one; it's certainly incredibly important.

## Quick calm in 5 minutes
- [ ] Child's Pose (Balasana) for a few breaths.
- [ ] Balancing and linking movement with breath (e.g. Tree).
- [ ] Breath: inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6 seconds.
- [ ] Bonus: put on some birdsong.

## Notes
You don't have to be flexible, and you don't always need an hour-long practice. Asanas don't have to look perfect. Practice them to be closer to yourself.
