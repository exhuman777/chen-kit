## How Agnieszka guides beginners

- [ ] **Breath before movement** — every session starts with a pause: comfortable seat, straight spine, closed eyes, attention only on the inhale and exhale. Movement always follows the breath (inhale opens, exhale rounds or deepens).
- [ ] **Zero pressure** — if your heels don't touch the mat in downward dog, "absolutely nothing bad happens". No need to grab your feet in a forward fold: "everything will come with time".
- [ ] **Props instead of forcing** — hand on the ankle, on a block or on books; a yoga strap, scarf or trouser belt in the seated forward fold; blanket, pillow, socks and a wall in the relaxing session.
- [ ] **Releasing tension** — constant reminders: relax the jaw, neck, shoulders, face, "that's where we tense up most often". And the refrain of the whole practice: "we don't forget to breathe".
- [ ] **Room for emotions** — in hip openers (pigeon): "sometimes you feel like crying — that's all okay".
- [ ] **Presence** — "we are here and now", thoughts "come and go like clouds in the sky", we don't analyze them. Every session ends with thanking yourself for the practice.

## Foundational poses with her cues

- [ ] **Cat–cow (tabletop)** — wrists under the shoulders, right angle between thighs and torso; inhale chest and gaze up, exhale round the back and tuck the pelvis.
- [ ] **Downward dog** — navel toward the spine, hips up; you can "pedal" the legs; later it becomes the resting pose.
- [ ] **Mountain pose** — feet pressed down with their whole surface, active legs, chest lifted, shoulders loose, belly engaged.
- [ ] **Warrior II and side bends** — front knee over the ankle, never past the toes; outer edge of the back foot pressed into the mat; hand on the ankle or a block, the other arm up.
- [ ] **Chair** — inhale lengthens the spine, exhale sits lower; tailbone gently tucked.
- [ ] **Plank and lowering** — elbows close to the body; for beginners the knees–chest–chin variant instead of a full lowering.
- [ ] **Pigeon** — strong hip opener; skip if the knee hurts, rest in a kneeling position.
- [ ] **Savasana** — "the most important pose of every sequence, we never neglect it". "Don't do anything at all now, just be. Trust that everything is exactly as it should be."

## Beginner videos

- [ ] **Session for beginners, 24 min** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SN_3CfS8C4A) — full sequence: seated breathing, cat–cow, downward dog, forward fold, warrior II with side bends on both sides, chair, plank, cobra, pigeon, seated forward fold, savasana. Agnieszka's tip: repeat the sequence 2–4 times when you need a longer session.
- [ ] **10-minute Cozy Session** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqBsyyI8qGY) — for a rainy, cold day: blanket, candle, soft light. Shoulder rolls, cat–cow, thread the needle, low lunge with side stretching, forward fold, legs up the wall with belly breathing, short savasana with a pillow and blanket.
- [ ] **37 poses for beginners, 14 min** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cwDByz-FB60) — silent demonstration of poses, no voice guidance. Instruction: hold each pose for at least 5 breaths, corpse pose (savasana) for 10, and repeat on the other side where needed.
- [ ] **Crow (Bakasana), 5 min** (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RsF7Z4r5oSQ) — preparation video: how to strengthen and warm up before crow pose.
