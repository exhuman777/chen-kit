# 7 Days Of Yoga — A Weekly Plan
category: yoga/plan
tags: yoga, plan, week, routine, home practice, YouTube
source: https://exhto.substack.com/p/7-dni-jogi

## Idea
A ready-made practice schedule for the whole week, built from sessions on the YouTube channel (Yoga Exhto), to help you feel wonderful. Each day has its own session — all you need is a mat.

## Plan
- [ ] **Monday:** Good Morning Flow — 14 minutes.
- [ ] **Tuesday:** a morning session to start the day; in the afternoon or evening, "A Day Off for Wrists and Hands".
- [ ] **Wednesday:** Gentle 15-minute sequence "Move Your Body" + get to know a breathing technique.
- [ ] **Thursday:** Morning Yoga Session.
- [ ] **Friday:** 60-minute yoga for a wonderful day.
- [ ] **Saturday:** start the day with meditation, then an 18-minute slow sequence (also works in the evening or after a workout).
- [ ] **Sunday:** Session for the shoulders.

## Notes
All sessions are available for free on the "Yoga Exhto" YouTube channel. You can loop the plan week after week.
