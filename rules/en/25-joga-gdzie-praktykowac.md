## Options
- [ ] **YouTube — the "Yoga Exhto" channel:** free sessions from 5 to 60 minutes — morning, evening, yin, for beginners, for shoulders and wrists, meditations.
- [ ] **Online 1:1 sessions (60 min)** and **sessions for couples (90 min)** — book through exhto.pl (booking tab).

## Credentials
- [ ] Agnieszka is a certified **RYT200** yoga teacher.

- [ ] **Evening and yin:** Evening Yoga (15 min), Yin Yoga for the nervous system (28 min), Yoga — 15 minutes of Relaxation, 18-minute slow sequence for a weekend morning or evening, Gentle 15-minute sequence "Move Your Body".

- [ ] **For beginners:** Yoga — session for beginners (24 min), 37 poses for beginners (14 min, demonstration — stay in each pose for at least 5 breaths), 10-minute Cozy Session.
- [ ] **Mindfulness and longer practices:** Yoga — 5 minutes of mindfulness, 60-minute yoga for a wonderful day.
- [ ] **Poses:** Crow (Bakasana) — how to strengthen and warm up before crow pose (5 min).

## Notes
For practicing at home, all you need is a mat and a quiet spot. The sessions are gentle and guided step by step, also for people starting from zero.
